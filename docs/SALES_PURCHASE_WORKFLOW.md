# SO → PO → bills → invoice → returns

Sales enters shop/customer, product SKU/name and quantity (canonical pcs), with salesperson, delivery address/date and unit sale price. Submitting SO now atomically checks free stock in Lebak → Kediri → Besar, reserves stock, and creates one automatic draft PO for shortages only. Fully stocked orders create no PO. Supplier is left blank for Admin 1 to choose, not guessed from SKU.

Admin 1 uses Purchasing to set supplier and destination warehouse, add/remove SKU rows and adjust qty/prices before saving. Authorization requires a supplier; only an authorized PO can be downloaded/printed. Authorized PO cannot be edited in place. Draft changes retain before/after history. PO dispatch remains manual. Current demo supports one PO/supplier per SO; split sourcing and partial/backorder cycles are not implemented.

Admin 1 records final vendor bills: each PO SKU defaults to zero, fill only supplied quantities. Missing/short quantities linked to customer demand become loss sales; extra procurement not ordered by the customer never becomes lost customer demand. Received purchases beyond demand remain free warehouse stock. Bills alone do not assert physical arrival: Admin 1 confirms goods received before stock increases, valuation updates and relevant SO reservations are added. Duplicate receipt is blocked.

After supplier final response and receipt, Admin 1 prepares final customer shipment/invoice from SO and available reservations. Remove SKU by setting shipment qty to zero (button provides reason), add catalog SKUs from free stock, or use the existing approved substitute flow. Added/replacement products require customer confirmation. Invoice creation and stock reduction commit together exactly once. Insufficient stock/invalid qty leaves state unchanged. Final unsent demand becomes loss without duplicating supplier loss. Source SO is retained; created invoice/shipment is immutable. Corrections after creation use returns, not silent line edits.

Customer returns: Sales submits linked invoice lines; Admin 2 chooses receiving warehouse and confirms physical receipt. Approval appends inventory receipt and customer credit, reducing invoice balance and reversing original HPP. Supplier returns: Admin 1 confirms goods returned from free stock; approval reduces stock and bill balance. Original invoices/bills remain unchanged. Existing return tests cover duplicate approval, entitlement limits, roles and atomic failures.

All records, simulated role controls and stock changes remain page-session demo data. Reload resets them. No database, authentication, vendor transmission, production ledger or payment integration. Invoice preview discount/tax/letterhead settings remain preview-only; workflow financial totals are merchandise values before preview taxes and discounts.

## Revised step 3: automatic editable bills

After PO authorization, Admin 1 sees a bill draft prefilled with every PO SKU, qty and price. Match it to supplier bills: edit qty/prices, remove unavailable SKU rows, or add catalog SKUs. Finalization compares every authorized PO row against the bills (including Admin 1 PO additions), records positive quantity shortages as loss sales, and records added/excess supplied goods without offsetting another SKU shortage. Empty bills represent full unavailability and create loss records without a zero-value bill. Finalized bills cannot be entered twice; stock still increases only at confirmed physical receipt. Draft bill form edits are unsaved until finalization and reset if the page is rerendered.

## Revised step 4: Admin 2 customer invoice

After stock allocation and required supplier receipt, Admin 2 opens a draft prefilled from original SO SKU/qty/prices. Add/remove catalog SKUs, reduce quantities to goods actually supplied, set discount 0–100%, choose A (DPP/PPN) or B (without PPN), then issue. Sales/Admin 1 cannot issue through this workflow. Issuance atomically records shipment/stock reduction and immutable invoice; invalid/insufficient stock and duplicate issuance fail without changes. Discounted unit sale prices are stored in the invoice, used by merchandise receivables, margin and return credits; SO prices are preserved. Gross prices and discount percentage are saved for print. The issued document locks discount and letterhead. At zero discount the print column is omitted. A stores DPP, PPN and amountDue; B amountDue equals net merchandise. Existing AR/AP/margin reports remain merchandise-based, not a posted tax ledger; a complete tax/payment ledger remains future work. Invoice draft form edits remain page-memory until issuance.

## Current business flow — latest revision

This section supersedes the earlier receipt and return-role descriptions above. Admin 1 enters PO supplier, discount percentage and payment terms before authorization. PO gross unit prices are retained; net unit prices use whole-rupiah half-up discount rounding and flow into the initial bills draft. Admin 1 may match final bills prices to actual vendor documents. Downloaded PO includes the discount and terms. Payment terms are descriptive, not a computed due date.

Finalizing bills confirms the supplied goods have arrived. Bill, loss sales, receipt, stock valuation and stock/reservation increases commit atomically; there is no second receipt action in the normal bills workflow. A repeated finalization/receipt is rejected. The draft form itself does not change stock. Customer invoice remains Admin 2 as previously described. Customer returns are submitted by Sales and authorized by Admin 1, who chooses the receiving warehouse; approved returns increase stock and reduce invoice balance. Vendor returns are created and confirmed by Admin 1 and reduce free stock and vendor bills. All remain client-side session records.

## Direct stock replenishment PO

Purchasing now provides an Admin 1-only PO form without SO: supplier, destination warehouse, SKU qty/prices, discount and payment terms. It creates a draft requiring normal Admin 1 authorization before download. It uses purpose stock_replenishment and null orderId; it creates no fake SO/customer, reservations or customer invoice. Authorized PO supplies the editable bills draft; finalization adds received SKU quantities as free stock atomically, updates weighted cost and bills/AP, and records every PO shortage as loss sales marked stock_replenishment. Shortage potential revenue uses current catalog selling price as an estimate, not a customer price commitment. Reports exclude null SO links from SO counts. These bills use the existing Admin 2 payment and Admin 1 vendor return workflows. Missing-all goods creates loss records without a zero-value bill or receipt; duplicate finalization is blocked.
