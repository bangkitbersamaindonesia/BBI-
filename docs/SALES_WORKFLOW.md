# SO → PO → supplier delivery/bill → customer delivery/invoice (demo)

## Workflow

1. **Sales** enters customer, salesperson, required date, address, optional notes and multiple SKU/qty/unit/price lines. Sales uses the same in-memory catalog as Inventory. Original SO lines, garment variants and IDR selling prices are snapshotted; duplicate SKU lines must be combined. Integer canonical pcs and safe integer rupiah calculations are validated. SO submission does not change physical stock.
2. **Admin 1** checks all warehouses (Lebak, Kediri, Besar). Available pcs means physical stock minus other existing reservations. Review allocates in that warehouse order and stores an availability snapshot. A fully allocated SO can go directly to customer delivery. A shortage becomes one linked draft PO, addressed to an explicitly entered supplier and receiving warehouse, for only the shortage lines.
3. **PO** can be marked ready and downloaded as demo text. Transmission occurs outside this application; no email/vendor integration or supplier authentication is implemented.
4. **Supplier final dispatch/bill** is recorded manually by Admin 1: final dispatch date, supplier bill reference, each SKU's shipped pcs, actual billed price per pcs, and reason for every unavailable remainder. The user confirms this is final and the remainder will not be sent. Pending responses/backorders must not be finalized. A bill includes only positive shipped quantities; unavailable quantities are excluded. If all are unavailable, no goods bill is created. Bill references are unique per supplier ignoring case. Records are unposted and exclude unconfirmed taxes, freight and discounts.
5. **BBI receipt** requires a separate explicit confirmation that all goods on the final supplier bill actually arrived in the PO's destination warehouse. One receipt adds only those pcs to physical balances and reserves them for the source SO. Repeated receipt is rejected. This demo does not handle receipt discrepancies or multiple supplier deliveries.
6. **Customer final dispatch** is recorded by Admin 1 on Sales, after required supplier response/receipt is complete. For each original SO line, record original SKU pcs sent and optionally one substitute SKU with pcs and agreed price. Original SKU comes from this SO's reservations; substitute uses free stock across warehouses and cannot consume another order's reservations. Substitution is 1 pcs for 1 pcs and requires explicit customer approval. Combined sent qty cannot exceed original ordered qty. A reason is required for any final unfulfilled balance. Pending/partial shipping must not be finalized in this demo.
7. **Customer invoice** is generated automatically as a draft from positive actual dispatch lines. It uses original SO prices for original SKUs and agreed prices for substitutes, retaining the source original SKU on each line. Unshipped quantities do not appear. Zero delivery creates no invoice. Original SO demand and total remain unchanged. Dispatch decrements warehouse pcs, writes stock movement records, releases unused reservations and freezes that final dispatch/invoice. It does not email invoices or post AR/GL, payment, COGS or stock valuation.

## Loss-sales records and analytics

A final unavailable supplier remainder creates a loss event linked to response, PO, original SO/SKU, customer, salesperson, supplier and destination warehouse. It snapshots garment name/size/color, date, pcs, reason and SO selling price. Initial warehouse shortage and pending supplier responses are not themselves loss sales.

Supplier nonavailability is preserved as gross history. When customer delivery uses a substitute, an append-only recovery record links the recovered pcs to the original loss and shipment. Gross loss records are not overwritten. If BBI fails to dispatch additional originally allocated goods, final delivery creates only the uncovered additional loss, attributed to BBI. No double counting of the supplier shortage occurs.

Reports presents gross shortage pcs, recovered substitute pcs, net loss pcs and net potential lost revenue. Net loss = gross shortage minus recovered pcs. Potential revenue uses original SO selling prices, not supplier costs, substitution revenue or profit; actual customer invoice uses actual dispatched SKUs/prices. Reports rank SKUs and show details, with inclusive event-date and SKU/supplier/customer filters. A recovered event stays in the gross history even when net is zero. Date filtering cohorts events by their original date; recovery status reflects latest dispatch, not a historical as-of balance. CSV exports the same filtered records with gross/recovered/net values, source references, reasons, quoted fields and formula escaping. Aggregates use BigInt to avoid precision loss.

## Demo limits and production work

All state is page-session memory and resets on reload. UI roles simulate Sales/Admin 1 and do not authenticate users. Histories, movements, receipts, bills, invoices and recovery events are demonstration objects, not protected persistent records or posted accounting. There are no executed database migrations.

Production requires server-side organization/role checks, decimal-safe prices, atomic reservations/stock writes, row locks, idempotency and durable append-only audit/reversal events. Freeze posted invoices; later corrections must use controlled revisions/credit notes rather than overwriting history. Confirm supplier/customer master data and contacts, manual allocation priority, reservation expiry/cancellation, multiple suppliers/bills/receipts, transit losses and receipt discrepancies, partial shipments, multiple substitutes and substitution ratios, attachment evidence, approval rules, tax/freight policies and actual document transmission before production implementation. This version has one final supplier response, one receipt, and one final customer dispatch/invoice per SO/PO.

`node --test` covers units, money limits, required fields and roles, cross-warehouse allocation and reservations, shortage-only POs, final supplier responses, partial/full/zero availability, duplicate recording, safe loss analytics/CSV, receipt idempotency, actual delivery invoice lines, approved substitutions, no overselling and gross/recovered/net loss reconciliation. `vite build` verifies integration. ## Customer and supplier returns

Forms appear under the original customer invoice in Sales and under the supplier bill in Purchasing. Sales or Admin 1 can submit a customer return; Admin 1 submits supplier returns. Each request contains the original document, exact document line(s), positive integer pcs, return date, requester, reason and warehouse. Supplier returns require an existing receipt. Pending and approved requests consume returnable quantity; rejected ones release it. A substitute item is returned as its actual invoiced SKU at its actual agreed price, retaining the original SO SKU reference. Two document lines with the same SKU remain distinct if prices differ.

Requests do not affect physical stock or document totals. Only Admin 1 can approve/reject. Approval requires a decision note and confirmation that customer goods have actually arrived and are fit for restocking, or that supplier goods have physically been returned and the bill reduction agreed. The demo performs these as one final approval, not a separate carrier/vendor integration. Rejection requires a note, releases the request's quantity hold and changes neither stock nor money.

Approval atomically appends a linked credit adjustment, stock movement(s), and audit event. Customer approval adds actual returned SKU pcs to the selected receiving warehouse; supplier approval subtracts pcs from the selected sending warehouse. Supplier returns recheck current free stock and cannot consume quantities reserved for a customer order. Duplicate approvals, negative stock, over-returned quantities, invalid warehouses, pre-source dates and unsafe totals are blocked before mutations. Multiple partial returns are supported within original document-line quantities.

Original invoice/bill lines and totals remain unchanged. Panels show original amount, approved return credits, and net amount after returns. Credits snapshot original line prices. Net amount cannot become negative. This is a goods-only demo before tax, freight or payment allocations; it does not post AR/AP/GL or automatically issue refunds. Returns are recorded separately from supplier-unavailability loss sales and do not silently rewrite historical loss or substitution recovery events. Whether returned substitutions should create additional commercial loss requires an explicit reporting rule.

Production needs durable return authorizations/receipts, stock condition/quarantine inspection, supplier agreement/credit evidence, atomic database transactions and immutable reversal accounting, paid-invoice overpayment/refund treatment, tax credit documents, inventory costing reversals and approval identity. No migration, authentication or real payment/transmission was added; session data still resets on reload.

## Gross margin per created invoice

Business rule: gross profit (IDR) = invoice goods sales − cost of goods sold. Per-line HPP uses the actual invoiced SKU's qty multiplied by its moving quantity-weighted average purchase cost at invoice creation. Gross margin % = gross profit / sales × 100; zero sales displays no percentage, and negative margins are allowed. The per-SKU pool combines physical stock across Lebak, Kediri and Besar. This adopts a company-wide per-SKU average for this demo, replacing the older proposed per-warehouse costing assumption. It is not a simple average of purchase prices.

Example: 100 pcs @ Rp80 plus receipt 50 pcs @ Rp92 gives 150 pcs costing Rp12,600, average Rp84. Invoice 20 pcs @ Rp100 has sales Rp2,000, HPP Rp1,680, gross profit Rp320, margin 16%. A substitute SKU uses its own average purchase cost. Only actual received supplier quantities update the pool; unreceived PO commitments and unavailable goods do not. Demo opening stock uses master purchase price as its explicit assumed opening cost. Production opening quantities/values must be imported and verified.

Invoice lines snapshot average cost and total HPP at creation. Later receipts, purchase-price changes and supplier returns never recalculate prior invoice margins. All goods receipts, customer dispatches and approved returns keep quantity and inventory value pools synchronized. Supplier returns remove current average stock value; their bill credit retains original supplier price, with the difference requiring a production variance policy. Customer returns restore original invoice HPP and reverse its cost, not the latest purchase cost. Both original and net-after-approved-returns margin are displayed on the invoice; Reports includes a separate table of all session invoices independent of loss-sales filters.

Cost arithmetic uses BigInt in integer millionths of IDR, serialized as strings; half-up division defines precision. Each line HPP is rounded once to integer IDR, then invoice HPP sums those rounded lines. Displayed unit averages can have six decimal places. Partial customer return HPP reversals use differences of cumulative proportional rounded source amounts so a full return reverses exactly the original HPP with no rounding drift. Percentages are display ratios rounded to two decimals. Missing historical cost snapshots are not estimated using current prices. The demo excludes taxes, freight/landed costs, GL postings and backdated revaluation; chronological processing, tax treatment and production precision remain to be finalized.
